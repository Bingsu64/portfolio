# Project ODYSSEY
This folder contains the PCB schematic and block documentation for a modular 5V power module designed for a modularized flight computer architecture 
intended for a Karman-Line, bi-liquid rocket system.  

Status: R&D / Preliminary Design Phase (Peer-Reviewed, Unmanufactured due to project budget constraints)

## ODYSSEY Avionics
Project ODYSSEY was an ambitious flagship initiative at ARIS aimed at developing a bi-liquid rocket to reach the Karman line (100km altitude).  
 - To support a complex, modularized flight computer architecture, reliable local power regulation was essential.
 - My specific ownership focused on designing the 5V power module block, ensuring stable power delivery and low noise sensitivity
 - Further focus on reliability by implementing redundancy, monitoring, and diagnostics capabilities for fault handling

## Design Readiness
Although the project concluded prematurely due to budget constraints and organizational restructuring, before physical manufacturing could take place.
The schematic underwent a rigorous formal peer-review with senior ARIS team members and technical leads. Based on review feedback, the documentation and net-labeling were drastically improved for legibility, component footprint verification, and manufacturability.

## Final Learnings
 - Upfront Design Rigor: Learned how to design power electronics strictly to specification and system requirements before physical hardware ever exists.
 - Design Reviews: Gained valuable experience defending schematic architecture, component choices, and safety margins during peer-review gates.
